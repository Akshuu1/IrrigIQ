# 🎙️ Pilot Interview Scripts: Farmers & Institutional Buyers

This guide provides structured interview questions for post-pilot evaluations. All responses must be recorded verbatim without leading prompts or subjective reinterpretation.

---

## Part 1: Farmer Structured Interview

**Target Participant**: Smallholder farmer who participated in the 5-week trial.  
**Interviewer**: Field Agent or Independent Evaluator.  
**Language**: Native language (Hindi, Marathi, Punjabi, or Telugu).

### A. Pre-Pilot Baseline & Traditional Practice
1. **Decision Making**: "Before this pilot, how did you decide when to start your pump? Did you follow a fixed calendar routine, watch the soil surface, or pump whenever electricity became available?"
2. **Pumping Hours**: "On average, how many hours per week did you run your tube-well/bore-well for this specific plot during this stage of the crop?"
3. **Power Scheduling**: "How did you manage rural 3-phase electricity hours? Did you have to wake up at night to turn on the pump?"

### B. Daily Advisory Usability & Clarity
4. **Timing & Channel**: "Did you receive the daily advisory message on your phone at an appropriate time in the morning? Was WhatsApp convenient, or would you prefer a regular phone call / SMS?"
5. **Clarity of Instructions**: "Was the advice easy to understand? Did the recommended pump run time in minutes make sense for your field size and pump capacity?"
6. **Scientific Reasons**: "Did you read the explanation provided (such as 'Rain expected' or 'Soil moisture trigger reached')? Did that reason make you trust the advice more?"

### C. Adherence & Field Realities
7. **Skipping Irrigation**: "When the advisory recommended 'Skip irrigation today', did you feel nervous that your crop would dry out? How did you verify the soil condition?"
8. **Reasons for Non-Adherence**: "On days when you could not follow the advice, what was the primary constraint? (e.g., no electricity during scheduled hours, pump failure, labor unavailable, or neighbor's field flooded your plot)?"
9. **Chat Diary Logging**: "Did you find it easy to reply with the number of minutes you pumped (e.g. sending '60' or '६०')? Did you receive an acknowledgment?"

### D. Economic Impact & Future Continuation
10. **Perceived Savings**: "Did you notice any difference in your electricity consumption, diesel bills, or pump wear-and-tear over the past month?"
11. **Crop Health**: "In your honest view, did the crop look just as healthy, better, or stressed compared to your traditional routine?"
12. **Willingness to Continue**: "Would you like to keep using IrrigIQ for the rest of this crop cycle? If a small fee (e.g. ₹30/month) were charged after the pilot, would you be willing to pay, or would you expect your cooperative/FPO to cover it?"

## Part 2: Institutional Buyer Structured Interview

**Target Participant**: Cooperative Director, FPO CEO, Agribusiness Procurement Manager, or Sustainability Lead.  
**Interviewer**: Pilot Lead / Project Director.

### A. Strategic Alignment & Value Proposition
1. **Organizational Goals**: "How does agricultural water accounting and groundwater conservation align with your organizational goals (e.g., ESG compliance, farmer livelihood improvement, raw material supply security)?"
2. **Farmer Trust & Extension**: "Did providing science-backed irrigation advisories strengthen your relationship and trust with your member farmers?"

### B. Operational Feasibility & Field Agent Workflow
3. **Agent Workload**: "Your field agents managed between 20 and 50 farmers each on the 'Today' mobile dashboard. How much time did they spend per day on advisory distribution and follow-ups? Was it manageable alongside their other duties?"
4. **Offline Resilience**: "Did the offline PWA functionality perform reliably in remote villages with poor network connectivity?"

### C. Commercial Viability & Cost-to-Serve
5. **Unit Economics**: "The calculated cloud infrastructure cost-to-serve is approximately ₹20–₹35 per farmer per month (including messaging, storage, and API services). Is this within your extension service budget or CSR/grant threshold?"
6. **Commercial Model**: "How would your organization fund this at scale? (e.g., funded by buyer/FPO, bundled with crop inputs, subsidized by a government grant, or shared with farmers)?"

### D. Governance, Reporting & Audit Rigor
7. **Impact Reporting**: "Did the automated bilingual PDF Impact Report and raw CSV telemetry export meet your requirements for executive review and external sustainability auditing?"
8. **Handling Caveats**: "The report explicitly states what observational pilots do not prove (i.e. not an RCT, baseline is self-reported, weather differences between periods). How was this honesty received by your leadership and donors?"
9. **Scaling Decision**: "What threshold of water savings or farmer satisfaction does your organization need to see before scaling this from the pilot cohort to your full membership (1,000+ farmers)?"
